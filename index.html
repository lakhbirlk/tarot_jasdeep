How a reading unfolds

    Love, career, a three-card spread, a yes/no question, an angel message or a live consultation.

Why a daily card helps

A daily card is less about prediction and more about attention. One card, read slowly, can surface a question you had been circling all week. Because it is the same card all day, it becomes something to return to rather than something to consume and forget.

The daily draw on this demo site is remembered in your browser, which means it can be reset. On a live site with accounts, a once-a-day limit would be enforced on the server instead.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is a tarot reading?

A tarot reading uses a deck of illustrated cards as a set of prompts for reflection. A reader lays cards out in a spread, and each position in that spread gives the card a particular meaning. The value of a reading comes from the thinking it encourages: what you notice, what you disagree with, and what you decide to do next.

How does an online tarot reading work?

You choose a reading category, write a short question or focus, and draw your cards from a face-down deck. The cards flip over with their names and illustrations, followed by an interpretation written for that position and an overall summary.

For a live consultation, you book a time and meet online or by phone, so there is space for a real conversation and follow-up questions.

Can I ask questions about love or career?

Yes — love and relationships, and career and finance, are two of the most common themes. Readings in both areas are reflective: they explore patterns, options and communication rather than promising a particular outcome.

A reading is not a substitute for professional advice. Legal, medical, financial and psychological decisions should be made with a qualified professional.

Do I need previous tarot experience?

Not at all. Each card is explained in plain language, and you are welcome to take what is useful and leave the rest. Many people use readings simply as a structured way to think something through.

How can I book a private session?

Use the booking form to share your name, contact details, the type of reading you would like, and a preferred date and time. You will be contacted to confirm the appointment.

Note for the site owner: the booking form on this static build does not send anything yet — it needs a backend or a form service before it can deliver real requests.

How do credits and payments work?

Each reading costs a set number of credits, and you buy credits in packages. On this demonstration site the wallet is simulated in your browser and no payment is taken at all. A live site would process payments through a payment provider and keep balances on a secure server, where they can be checked independently of the browser.

Is tarot a guaranteed prediction of the future?

No. Nothing on this site should be understood as a guaranteed or certain prediction. Tarot cards do not determine what happens, and a reading cannot tell you what another person will do.

Readings here are offered as spiritual interpretation and personal reflection, for entertainment and insight. They are not legal, medical, financial or psychological advice, and they do not replace the judgement of a qualified professional.
