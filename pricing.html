Before you buy

How payments would work on a live site

A static website cannot take payments safely. Payment details must never be handled by front-end JavaScript, and a credit balance kept in a browser can be edited by anyone using developer tools.

  1. You choose a package

    You are sent to the payment provider's own secure checkout, hosted by them, not by this site.

  2. The provider confirms the payment

    The provider notifies the server directly. Only then are credits added to your account.

  3. Your balance lives on the server

    Each reading is checked against the server-side balance, so a balance cannot be altered in the browser.

  4. You get a receipt

    Invoices, refunds and cancellations are handled through the provider and the site's published policy.

Not yet connected

Everything in the list below still needs to be built or configured before this site can sell readings. The demo wallet on this page deliberately does none of it.

  • A payment provider account (for example Razorpay or Stripe), subject to eligibility
  • A server that verifies payments and stores balances and reading history
  • User accounts with secure password handling and email verification
  • Tax, invoicing and refund handling for the countries you sell in
  • Server-side enforcement of the daily tarot limit and per-account reading history

The README that ships with this project lists these items in detail.

Pricing questions

Credits, refunds and what happens next

Do credits expire?

In this demo, credits stay until you reset the wallet or clear your browser data. Any real expiry policy would be set by the site owner and written into the terms and refund policy before launch.

What if I do not have enough credits?

The site will tell you how many credits the reading costs and how many you have, and offer a link to the packages on this page. No reading is started and nothing is deducted.

Can I get a refund?

That depends on the policy published at launch. See the Refund and Cancellation Policy, which currently holds a template for the site owner to complete.

Is my card stored anywhere?

No card details are collected on this website at all. Payment details are never entered into a page like this one; they belong on the payment provider's own secure checkout.
