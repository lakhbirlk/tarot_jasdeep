About Dr. Jasdeep Kaur

The person behind the readings

Professional background

Dr. Jasdeep Kaur holds a PhD in Law and has worked as a practising advocate for over eleven years. Her professional life has been built around reading situations carefully, listening closely, and helping people understand the options in front of them.

  • PhD in Law

    Doctoral research in law — a legal qualification, not a spiritual or tarot one.

  • Practising advocate — 11+ years

    Over a decade of professional legal practice, working directly with people at difficult moments in their lives.

  • Tarot and spiritual guidance

    A separate personal practice, offered on this website as reflective card reading and gentle spiritual guidance.

These two things are kept separate. A PhD in Law and eleven years as an advocate are legal qualifications. They are not tarot, astrology or spiritual qualifications, and they do not make a reading legal advice. What to expect

How a reading works here

Sessions are held online or by phone, at a time you choose. You are welcome to bring one question or several. There is no script to follow and no need to prepare anything in advance.

  • Private and respectful. What you discuss stays between you and Dr. Jasdeep Kaur.
  • Reflective, not predictive. Cards are used to open up thinking, not to promise outcomes.
  • Your decisions stay yours. A reading offers perspective; it does not make choices for you.
  • No pressure. You can stop the session at any point, or ask to skip a topic.

Questions people often ask first

Is this confidential?

Yes. Readings are private, and nothing you share is published or passed on.

Do you offer legal advice?

No. Legal work and tarot reading are separate practices. Nothing on this website is legal advice, and a reading cannot be used in place of professional legal, medical, financial or psychological support.

Can I ask about someone else?

Readings focus on your situation and your choices. Questions about other people's private lives or intentions are reframed around what is within your control.
