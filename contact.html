Quick answers

Before you write to us

How do I book a reading?

Use the booking form. It asks for your preferred date and time, the type of reading, and how you would like to meet.

I did not receive a reply yet — what should I do?

Please check your spam folder first, then write again. The published response time will be added to this page once the studio confirms it.

Can I ask a question about my reading afterwards?

Yes. Follow-up questions after a consultation are welcome, and a longer session can be arranged if you would like to go further into something.

Do you read for people outside India?

Sessions are held online or by phone, so location is rarely a barrier — but please confirm the studio's availability in your time zone when booking.
