2. What this website currently collects

In its current form this is a static website. It has no server-side application, no database and no user accounts. As a result:

  • Nothing you type into a form is transmitted anywhere. The booking, contact, login and registration forms validate your input in the browser and then tell you they have not sent it.
  • No payment information is collected at all. There is no checkout on this site.
  • Passwords are never stored — not in localStorage, sessionStorage, cookies or anywhere else.

3. Information stored in your own browser

Some features work by saving small amounts of data in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device, is not readable by this website's owner, and disappears if you clear your browser data:

  • djk.demo.wallet.v1 — your demonstration credits and demo transaction history.
  • djk.demo.readings.v1 — readings you chose to save, including the question you typed.
  • djk.demo.daily.v1 — the date and card of your daily draw.
  • djk.demo.bookingDraft.v1 — a draft of the booking form, so a page refresh does not lose what you had typed. It is removed after a demo submission.

You can remove all of it at any time from your browser settings, or with the "Reset demo wallet" button on the Pricing & Credits page.

4. Cookies and third-party requests

This website sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. It does load two external resources, which means your browser contacts those providers directly:

  • Google Fonts — supplies the typefaces used on every page. Google receives your IP address and user agent as part of that request. If you prefer to avoid this, the fonts can be self-hosted.
  • Your hosting provider — like almost all web hosts, will record standard server logs such as IP address, timestamps and requested pages. Retention for those logs is [to be completed].

No payments are processed on this website, and no card or bank details are ever entered here. When a payment provider is connected (for example Razorpay or Stripe, subject to eligibility), that provider will handle payment data under its own privacy policy, and only a payment reference and status would be stored on the server.
