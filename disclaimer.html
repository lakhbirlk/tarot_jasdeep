Disclaimer

Please read this before booking a reading. It explains plainly what a reading can and cannot do.

In one sentence: readings on this website are reflective and offered for entertainment — they are not predictions, and they are not professional advice of any kind.

1. Readings are reflective, not predictive

Tarot and angel card readings use illustrated cards as prompts for reflection. They cannot tell you what will happen, cannot tell you what another person is thinking or will do, and cannot guarantee any outcome. Anything that sounds like a prediction should be understood as a possibility to consider, not a fact about the future.

2. No professional advice

Nothing on this website — and nothing said in a reading — is legal, medical, psychological, psychiatric, financial, investment or tax advice. Readings are not a diagnosis and not a treatment.

If you are facing a legal matter, a health concern, a mental health difficulty or a significant financial decision, please consult a qualified professional. If you are in crisis or at risk of harm, contact your local emergency services or a crisis helpline immediately. A tarot reading is not the right support for that situation.

3. Legal qualifications are separate from spiritual practice

Dr. Jasdeep Kaur holds a PhD in Law and has practised as an advocate for over eleven years. Those are professional legal qualifications and experience. They are not tarot, astrology or spiritual qualifications, and they do not make a tarot reading legal advice. Nothing on this website is legal advice, and using this website does not create any lawyer–client relationship.

4. Your decisions remain yours

A reading may offer a perspective you had not considered, but the decisions you take afterwards are yours. Please do not delay seeking proper professional advice because of something a card seemed to suggest.

5. Age

This website and its readings are intended for adults aged 18 and over.

6. What a reader cannot do

A reading will not, under any circumstances:

  • provide information about another person's private life, health, or intentions;
  • offer a diagnosis, cure, or any form of medical or psychological treatment;
  • promise that a specific outcome — financial, romantic, legal or otherwise — will occur;
  • replace the judgement of a qualified professional in any field.

7. Demonstration features on this website

The interactive reading, the daily card, the credits wallet and the forms on this website are a front-end demonstration. Card selection is randomised in your browser, readings are not reviewed by a person, credits are simulated, and the forms do not send anything. Content such as prices, durations, contact details and availability are placeholders until the site owner confirms them.

8. Accuracy of information

Reasonable care is taken to keep the information on this website accurate, but no warranty is given that everything is complete or current. Where something matters to you, please confirm it before relying on it.

9. External links

Any external links are provided for convenience. This website is not responsible for the content, policies or practices of other sites.
