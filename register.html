What an account would give you

  • Credits held securely on the server, not in a browser
  • A history of readings you can return to from any device
  • Booking confirmations and reminders by email
  • One daily card per day, genuinely enforced

How it must be built

  • Passwords hashed with a modern algorithm (for example bcrypt or Argon2)
  • Email verification before the account becomes active
  • Server-side validation and rate limiting on every endpoint
  • HTTPS everywhere, with secure, http-only session cookies
