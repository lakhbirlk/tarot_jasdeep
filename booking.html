Before you book

Booking questions

How soon will I hear back?

That depends on the studio's published response time, which the site owner should add to this page along with the real business hours. Please do not treat the placeholder hours in the footer as confirmed.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Once a real booking system is in place, rescheduling and cancellation will follow the Refund and Cancellation Policy. That policy is currently a template that the site owner needs to complete.

What should I prepare?

Nothing in particular. A rough sense of what you would like to look at is helpful, and it is fine to arrive with a question you have not been able to put into words yet.

Do I have to use credits to book a consultation?

On this demo, credits only apply to the automated readings in the interactive reader. A live consultation is arranged directly, and its fee would be confirmed with you before the appointment.

Is a reading a substitute for professional advice?

No. Readings are reflective and offered for entertainment. They are not legal, medical, financial or psychological advice. Please see the Disclaimer for the full statement.
