Tarot Readings & Spiritual Guidance

Six ways to work with the cards, each one written to help you think something through. Every reading is reflective — a source of perspective, never a guarantee about what will happen.

Love & Relationship Tarot

For questions about a partner, a situationship, a friendship, a family bond, or the relationship you have with yourself. This reading reflects on emotional patterns, communication and the choices that are genuinely yours to make.

  • Where your heart is now — what you are carrying into this
  • The connection between you — the dynamic as it stands
  • Gentle guidance — what may help from here
20 credits · approx. 15 min Start Reading

Career & Finance Tarot

For decisions about work, study, business, money and long-term plans. The reading reflects on direction, opportunity and the trade-offs in front of you — it is not financial advice, and it will not tell you what to invest in.

  • Your current situation — what is really in play
  • The opportunity or challenge — what deserves attention
  • A suggested next step — one practical move
20 credits · approx. 15 min Start Reading

Three-Card Tarot Reading

The most flexible spread there is, and the best place to start if you are new to tarot. Three cards trace a line from what has shaped the situation to where it may be heading.

  • Past — what brought you here
  • Present — what is true right now
  • Possible direction ahead — one route, not a fixed ending
15 credits · approx. 10 min Start Reading

Yes / No Tarot

One focused question, one card. Rather than a flat answer, you get a lean — towards yes, towards no, or clearly undecided — plus the nuance behind it and what to weigh up before you decide.

  • Best for a single, specific question
  • Comes with a clear note on what the card does and does not say
  • Ends with a reflection prompt, not an instruction
10 credits · approx. 5 min Start Reading

Angel Card Reading

Angel card reading is used here as an inspirational practice: a message of encouragement, hope and mindfulness, drawn from a deck of gentle, affirming cards. It is a softer form of reading, and it makes no claim to channel a specific being or to deliver a guaranteed message.

  • A short message to sit with
  • A reflection question for the day
  • An affirmation you can carry with you
10 credits · approx. 5 min Start Reading

Personalized Tarot Consultation

A one-to-one session online or by phone, with time for the whole story rather than a single question. This is the option to choose when the situation has layers, or when you would simply rather talk it through with someone.

  • 45 minutes, focused entirely on your situation
  • Follow-up questions welcome during the session
  • Booked at a date and time you choose
60 credits · approx. 45 min Request a session
